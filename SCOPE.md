# Wildbound GBA-style reboot — Scope Gate v0.1

**Reference:** The feel of FireRed / LeafGreen era top-down exploration and turn-based monster battle. **Goal:** Create our *own* small, commercially shippable creature RPG, not a ROM hack or imitation of Nintendo-owned characters and maps.

**Prototype acceptance:** One starter choice; walk to tall grass; turn-based fight; catch creature; change leader; level and evolve; save and resume.

**No** prestige, idle economy, egg-hatch gambling, ads, IAP, large open world, online play, dozens of species, or procedural terrain in this milestone.

**Later, only after core fun:** a small quest hub, 1–2 more routes, 6–10 original species, one scripted trainer battle, a creature journal, healing and storage. Monetization separately validated against audience/platform norms. A Gen III-inspired visual direction does NOT mean copying maps, sprite pixels, battle screens, dialogue, franchise creatures, or branded terminology.
